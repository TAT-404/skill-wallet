# 内置封面

`covers.png` 是用内置 image_gen 工具生成的六格封面图集，已嵌入程序，不需要网络。WPF ImageBrush 读取图集中对应区域，不生成或修改用户正文。六种主题依次为产品精修、研究、写作、开发、学习、商业。界面截图是实际 WPF 控件渲染，不是用概念图作为背景。

生成提示词：

Create ONE production asset atlas of six photographic card covers, not a UI screenshot. Perfect uniform 3 columns by 2 rows, each panel equal size, borderless edge-to-edge with no gutters and no rounded corners. Overall landscape 2:1 ratio, each panel 4:3. NO labels, NO readable text, NO watermarks. Photorealistic premium editorial studio photography, rich saturated materials and controlled soft illumination. Each subject centered in own panel. TOP LEFT: champagne-silver over-ear headphones on warm ivory background with a small translucent orange acrylic cube at right, product fully visible. TOP MIDDLE: vivid green leaf macro with dew drops and a few ivory research-paper sheets to the right with illegible fine typographic marks, rich green backdrop. TOP RIGHT: textured ivory paper lying diagonally on sunshine-yellow background, elegant black abstract calligraphic flourish (no word) and black-gold fountain pen. BOTTOM LEFT: cobalt-blue glass programming screen with fine abstract blue code-like marks, no legible text, clean dark-blue technology still life. BOTTOM MIDDLE: sculptural folded white paper geometry and pale ice-blue note cards on frosty blue backdrop. BOTTOM RIGHT: violet translucent architectural glass blocks arranged as an ascending business chart against deep plum backdrop. Six independent scenes with clean vertical boundaries at exactly 1/3 and 2/3 canvas width, horizontal boundary at exactly 1/2 canvas height. Nothing spans panels. All panels composition safe for cropping to a portrait card top section. Premium coherent image collection. Atlas only.
